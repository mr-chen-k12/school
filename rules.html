Classroom Expectations & Behavioral Agreement

Building a safe, respectful, and growth-minded community in Mr. Chen's class

1

Core R.I.S.E. Values

R

Respect

Keep hands, feet, and personal space to yourself. Treat classmates and teachers with kind, respectful words.

I

Integrity

Take ownership of your choices, submit honest original work, and do the right thing even when no one is looking.

S

Safety — Non-Negotiable

Physical and emotional safety are mandatory. Physical aggression, harassment, and bullying are not permitted.

E

Effort & Perseverance

Give your best effort and persist through academic challenges. Repeated missing work may lead to Lunch Support with Mr. Chen.

2

Safety Non-Negotiables & Privileges

Physical & Emotional Safety Rules

Participation in field trips, grade-level events, and special privileges is an earned trust. Students must consistently demonstrate safe behavior around peers and staff. Repeated safety violations may result in the immediate loss of privileges and an administrative review of future eligibility.

3

Language Policy & Support Pathways

Classroom Speech Standard

Vulgarity, profanity, slurs, and culturally hurtful or offensive speech are prohibited in our learning environment.

Minor Vocal Slip-Ups

The first response is verbal redirection. Continued slip-ups may lead to a seat reassignment and family contact.

Severe or Culturally Offensive Words

A first occurrence may bypass warnings and lead directly to family contact and school-administrator intervention.

Step 1Verbal redirection
Step 2Family contact and support
Step 3Administrator and family conference
4

Academic Accountability & Homework Support

Building Responsibility

Students are expected to complete and submit assignments on time. A student who consistently misses work or needs additional guidance may attend Lunch with Mr. Chen, or another available teacher, to receive coaching, catch up, and build productive habits.

5

Student & Family Agreement

I understand and agree to the classroom rules, expectations, safety non-negotiables, language policies, and privilege guidelines for Mr. Chen's Grade 6 mathematics class.
